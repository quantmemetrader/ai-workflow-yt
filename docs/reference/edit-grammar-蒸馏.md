## Pacing

- Visual changes (layout, footage or graphic): 71 in 275 s → one every 3.9 s on average; longest stretch without a change 8 s (a head run), shortest 2 s.
- Share of time: head 47 % · PiP over footage 48 % · other (money-rain overlay, end card) 5 %.
- PiP runs are 4–14 s and always return to the head; head runs are 4–12 s. The alternation is the rhythm.
- Footage inside a PiP run changes every 2–4 s (3–4 stock clips per run).
- Every named thing gets a picture within the same second it is said: NSA/CISA (seals), the advisory (screenshot), Anthropic (its CEO on stock), the 商务部 statement (screenshot), ByteDance (logo card), 张一鸣 (interview footage), Kimi (phone), dollars (bills), servers, chips.

- Caption keyword highlights: 1–2 per line on roughly half the lines; whole lines in accent when the line is a claim (`一轮比一轮官方`).
